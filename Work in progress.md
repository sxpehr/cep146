# Exercise 3: Seneca Academic Integrity Policy

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to promote a culture of **academic honesty**, **trust**, **fairness**, **respect**, and **responsibility** at Seneca Polytechnic.

This policy explains the expectations related to academic integrity and outlines the responsibilities of students, faculty, and staff.

---

## Definition of Academic Integrity

> Academic integrity is the practice of honesty and responsibility in scholarship. Students are expected to submit original work and properly acknowledge the ideas and contributions of others.

---

# Policy Sections

## 1. General

- Students must complete their own academic work.
- Cheating, plagiarism, and falsification are violations of academic integrity.
- Students are responsible for understanding academic integrity expectations.
- Faculty members are responsible for reporting violations.

### Examples of Academic Misconduct

- Copying another student’s work
- Using unauthorized materials during a test
- Submitting purchased assignments
- Improper citation of sources
- Falsifying documents or records

---

## 2. Academic Integrity Program

The Academic Integrity Program supports students and faculty through:

- Educational workshops
- Learning resources
- Policy awareness activities
- Guidance on proper citation and referencing

### Program Goals

- Promote ethical learning
- Encourage responsible academic behavior
- Reduce incidents of academic dishonesty

---

## 3. Responsibilities

### Student Responsibilities

- Submit original work
- Cite all sources properly
- Follow instructions during tests and exams
- Ask questions if unsure about academic rules

### Faculty Responsibilities

- Explain academic integrity expectations clearly
- Report suspected violations
- Provide fair and consistent evaluations

### College Responsibilities

- Maintain fairness in academic procedures
- Support students with learning resources
- Apply sanctions consistently

---

## 4. Sanctions

Students who violate the Academic Integrity Policy may receive penalties depending on the severity of the offense.

Possible sanctions include:

- Warning or reprimand
- Reduction of grades
- Grade of zero on an assignment or test
- Failure in a course
- Suspension from the college
- Expulsion from the college

**Note:** Repeated violations may result in more serious disciplinary action.
